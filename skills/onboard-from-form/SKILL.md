# Onboard From Form — capture client data into the shared backend

Takes the §9b onboarding inputs (owner-filled + agency-set) and writes them into the ONE shared backend as a new `clients` row + related config. This is data capture/mapping, not site generation. Every write is server-side (admin client); the owner never writes directly to the DB.

**AS-BUILT — the C-3 onboarding wizard [BUILT + validated; `cloud-spark-setup` `origin/main`, 2026-06-28].** The capture surface is now real: an **8-step stepper** at `/onboard` (`src/routes/_authenticated/onboard.tsx`, admin/`agency_owner`-gated) that assembles `{ slug, fields, sendSettings, submission }` and calls `createClientFull`. **[C-3d-1]** The wizard is now **mode-aware** — extracted to `OnboardWizard` (`src/components/onboard/OnboardWizard.tsx`), rendered in **agency mode** by this authed `/onboard` route OR in **client mode** on the **public `/onboard/$token`** route (a one-time tokenized link the agency generates + emails; see `agency-view` + the `onboarding_tokens` model in `scratch-foundation`). Client-mode **uploads are LIVE [C-3d-2]** via a token-gated service-role proxy (`/api/public/onboarding/upload` → `uploadSiteImageViaProxy`; validates the token → writes to `public-assets` under the token's `draft_id`; per-token/IP rate-limit + image/10MB caps). Agency mode keeps the direct `uploadSiteImage`. **[C-3d-3] Client submit is LIVE** — client mode posts to the public **`/api/public/onboarding/submit`** route (token **claim-first** → shared **`insertClientFull`** forces `status='pending'` server-side, slug-collision auto-resolved → back-links `created_client_id`); the client sees a mode-aware success screen (**"All Set! — you'll receive login details soon"**, no add-another/Admin link). The submitted pending client flows into the agency Onboarding queue → `/admin/review` → Finalize & Invite. **[BACKLOG] Turnstile on the public submit is deliberately NOT built** (single-use token + rate-limit + private one-time links to known clients suffice); revisit only if onboarding becomes publicly accessible. **[C-3c-2a]** `createClientFull` creates the row as **`status='pending'`** (dormant — no automations / not anon-visible until the agency runs **Finalize & Invite** in `/admin`, which flips it to `active`). **App-layer only — no schema, no migration** (everything lands in existing `clients` columns / `template_vars` / `send_settings` / `public-assets`). The field→destination table below is the implemented contract. Steps as built:
1. **Account Setup** — owner full name; business name; **"Personal Cell / Business Number"** (US mask `(305) 555-0142`, **no country-code input**, stored **E.164 `+1…`** via `toE164US` on assembly → `call_forwarding_number`); display address; **"Website Domain"** (+ "I don't have a domain" → preferred-domain field + "get me the closest" button, recorded to `submission.domain_request`); business notification email. **Slug is HIDDEN** — auto-derived from the business name (`slugify`) and still sent in the payload.
2. **Content** — **Business Industry / Niche** (free-text → `template_vars.segment`; moved here when the Industry & Style step was removed 2026-07-22); About Us / Services / Differentiators; **service areas** (CSV, max 14 → `service_area[]`); **per-day business-hours picker** (all 7 days default OPEN 09:00–17:00, toggle a day Closed) → **`clients.hours` (PUBLIC)** with **`send_settings.business_hours` DERIVED EQUAL** at onboarding; **timezone** select (moved here from the old Config step).
3. **Branding** — **logo image upload** → `public-assets` via `src/lib/clients/site-image-upload.ts`, sets `clients.logo_url`; OR "I don't have a logo" → "make one for me? Yes/No" (`submission.logo_request`). **Brand colors via native `<input type="color">` pickers** (primary/secondary/tertiary; `ColorField` = color swatch + hex text input); OR "not sure — we'll choose for you" (`submission.color_request`).
4. **Industry & Style — STEP REMOVED (2026-07-22).** Niche/segment moved to the top of Content (step 2). The client-facing **site-style choice is GONE**: the agency picks the template shell for every client OUTSIDE the app, and all `siteStyle` plumbing was deleted from the wizard / server fns / admin Settings / FinalizeInvite (the `clients.site_style` column survives as a dormant golden-master column — never written, never read).
5. **Photos** — **multi-file categorized image uploads** to `public-assets` via `site-image-upload.ts`: `work` / `gallery` / `about` / `services`, plus **staff** (per entry an **individual/group toggle** — individual = photo + name + position; group = photo + label) → assembled into the **`template_vars.site_assets` manifest** (shape in `/website-structure`). "Don't have photos — design this section for me" → `submission.photo_request.designForMe`.
6. **Social Links** (renamed from "Reviews") — the client's **Google Business Profile link** (agency lookup) → **`template_vars.google_business_profile_link`** (+ immutable copy in `submission`) **PLUS Instagram / Facebook / LinkedIn** URLs (public, "shows on your website") → **`clients.social_links`** (jsonb `{platform: url}`, via `ClientFields.socialLinks` → `clientPatch`; renders in the site footer). **Each field (incl. GBP) has an "I don't have one" box** that omits that platform. The wizard still **does NOT set `clients.review_link` or `template_vars.review_request_link`** — the formatted Google review URL is **agency-set in admin Settings** (`review_request_link` synced from it on save). Star threshold / review-gate toggle / Place ID **removed from the form** (DB defaults: `star_threshold=4`, `google_review_toggle=gated`).
7. **Texting Registration (A2P)** — **required to advance: EIN, legal business name, legal business address, vertical/industry, TCPA attestation**; **DBA optional**; **Entity type REMOVED** (always the same for us). Captured into the immutable `submission` JSON (PII) + `template_vars` (anon-safe); **the legal business address ALSO writes to the new `clients.legal_address` column** — the registered business address exactly as it appears on the client's IRS documents (required for 10DLC brand/campaign registration; **MAY DIFFER from the public business address shown on the website**) — **and is editable in `/admin-view` Settings** so the agency can confirm/correct it against the actual IRS documents before registration; `a2p_status` stays `not_started` — the wizard PREPARES, never submits. **[2026-07-22] Optional EIN Confirmation Letter (CP 575) upload** directly under the EIN field ("Recommended … so we can double-check what you entered against your official documents. (Not required, but recommended.)") — PDF/image ≤10MB → PRIVATE `client-assets/{draftId}/a2p/ein-letter.*` (client mode via the token proxy's `docType='ein_letter'` whitelist — the ONLY docType routed to client-assets; pdf allowed for it only), moved to `client-assets/{clientId}/a2p/` on submit, path recorded in the submission JSON; agency views it via a 5-min signed URL in `/admin/review` + Settings (admin-gated fn — never publicly readable). Operationalizes the documents-first rule: registration identity is cross-checked against the actual IRS letter.
8. **Review & Create** — a **readable, grouped proofread summary** (sections in wizard order: Account Setup / Content / Branding / Photos / Social Links / Texting Registration), replacing the old raw-JSON dump (`src/components/onboard/ReviewSummary.tsx`): formatted phone, per-day hours list, friendly timezone/style names, brand hex swatches, plain-language request flags, photo counts per category, staff entries with thumbnails. The **Create** button assembles the **unchanged** payload and calls `createClientFull`. *(D7: this final review step IS the pre-gen console.)*

**Removed from the form** vs the old spec (agency-set later / DB defaults apply): SMS send window, daily send + enrollment caps, marketing domains, quote-form link, terms link, **entity type**, **Place ID**, **star threshold**, **review-gate toggle**, the standalone **Config step**, the **visible slug input**, the **Returning Customer Discount step** (moved to agency-set admin Settings), and the **direct Google review link** (now agency-set; onboarding collects the Google Business Profile link instead). Also removed 2026-07-22: the **site-style choice** (agency picks the template shell outside the app; nothing written to `clients.site_style`).

This is the per-client capture surface (was specced as "Stage 3 built / Stage 5 used"; now BUILT as **Phase C-3** — C-3a stepper + R-1 copy/structure + R-2 uploads/manifest + R-3 review summary, all UI-validated).

## Two input layers (§9b)
- **Owner-filled** — the onboarding form the business owner completes (content + brand).
- **Agency-set** — config you set during setup (review link, messaging-provider (Telnyx default) number/A2P, domains, etc.).

## template_vars — the single source for merge values [LOCKED]
All per-client merge values live in `clients.template_vars` (jsonb), NOT as dedicated columns. Required keys (RED — must be set before finalize; == `src/lib/admin/required-template-vars.ts`, **8 keys** since 2026-07-23): `company_owner_first_name`, `company_name`, `review_request_link`, `discount__on_referral`, `discount_amount`, `about_us`, `services`, `differentiators` (the last three are the AI-knowledge content fields — F-complete Option A, anon-safe public content). NOT required (all removed from the required list 2026-07-23): `company_website_link` = post-launch YELLOW, set at domain-live D.5 (a no-prior-domain client leaves it blank at onboarding); `quote_form_link` = **fallback-only, no Settings editor** — blank auto-derives from `company_website_link` at render (used only in Missed-Call Textback SMS #1); `website_terms_page_link` = site terms link only, may stay blank (A2P registration runs on a separate external platform). (review_request_link is the client's own direct Google review link, distinct from the per-contact tracked `review_link`.) Validate the required keys before the client goes live — missing keys render blank silently in messages.

**SECURITY [LOCKED]:** `template_vars` is **ANON-READABLE** — it is projected by the §A **`get_client_public` RPC** (the marketing site reads merge values via the RPC; there is NO `clients_public` view). So it holds **anon-safe merge values ONLY** — NEVER owner PII (notification email), secrets, or internal config. Owner-PII config (notification recipient email, etc.) belongs in a **dedicated `clients` column NOT projected by `get_client_public`** (anon-denied, 42501, same posture as `clients.email` / `call_forwarding_number`). *(3g initially put `notification_email` in `template_vars` — moved out; the RPC also defensively strips it from the projection. See `stage-3g-validation.md` F-pii.)*

## Build notes
- All writes via the admin (service-role) client in a server function — never owner-direct-to-DB. Zod-validate inputs.
- `call_forwarding_number` precedence: the owner's **Business Phone seeds it as the default**; the **agency value wins if both set** (agency configures the actual forwarding target in admin, editable over time).
- Creating the client today: `createClient` accepts only slug/business_name/phone_display/email; everything else here is set via an extended onboarding capture / `updateClientSettings` (extend it to cover all §9b fields — [BUILD]).
- Assets: logo + public hero images → `public-assets` (public read); private uploads → `client-assets` (client_id-scoped).
- A2P terms page (§9b.C) is generated as part of onboarding and its URL stored in `template_vars.website_terms_page_link`.
- Telephony setup (§9b.D) — the per-client messaging-provider flow (default **Telnyx**: 10DLC Brand → Campaign → number → Messaging Profile → TeXML app; legacy **TextGrid**: subaccount → Brand → Campaign → number; plus forwarding + GBP/site placement) — is an onboarding step (orchestrated by `/new-client-site`, detail for the default path in `skills/telnyx-provider` §4; the legacy TextGrid path in `skills/textgrid-provider`); this skill just records the resulting number/SID — `telnyx_number` (+ the `telnyx_*` IDs) on Telnyx, or `twilio_number`/`messaging_service_sid` on the legacy TextGrid path.
- **A2P field coverage (FLAG):** the per-client Brand/Campaign registration (TextGrid §4) needs EIN ✓ (captured), legal business name ✓, website ✓, **legal business address ✓ (`clients.legal_address` — from the client's IRS documents; captured at wizard Step 7 + editable in Settings; MAY DIFFER from the public `clients.address`)**, the opt-in/T&C URL ✓ (generated terms page), and the `vertical`/industry ✓ (**captured at onboarding wizard Step 7** — "vertical/industry" is required to advance; also derivable from the client's niche/`segment`). **A2P-prep field capture + compliance-copy generation → `/a2p-site-compliance`** (canonical verbatim copy: `docs/a2p-compliance-copy-source-of-truth.md`): legal name/DBA/entity type (default Private Profit)/segment→niche-key/EIN+issuer/DUNS-GIIN-LEI/legal business address (`clients.legal_address`)/website/contact-email **domain-match-enforced**/E.164 phone/business description/logo ≤400px/TCPA attestation/business-domain-email flag. The site's opt-in/Privacy/ToS/SMS-Program copy is reproduced VERBATIM from that doc (tokens only).
- **Immutable onboarding-submission record [BUILD — data-only, migration-free]:** preserve the RAW as-submitted answers + logo as a read-only record, distinct from the editable pre-generation config. Store the submission JSON in the existing private `client-assets` bucket at `{client_id}/onboarding-submission.json` (NOT in `template_vars` — it carries PII and must stay immutable + anon-private); the authenticated admin reads it into a read-only viewer (`/admin-view`). The editable config (prefilled FROM the submission) writes to the `clients` row / `template_vars` as usual. No new table/column.
- Content captured here (plus the agency's own style pick, made outside the app) are the inputs `/website-structure` consumes to generate the site.
