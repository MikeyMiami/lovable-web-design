## Read-only-verify findings (template @ f16e186)
The home route `src/routes/index.tsx` is **bespoke** (does NOT use `ContentPageView`). What's already CMS-driven vs the gaps:
- **H1 — already CMS-driven.** `const h1 = page?.h1 ?? t.hero_headline ?? c.business_name` (`index.tsx:41`) → the CMS home `h1` (primary category + city) renders in the styled hero. ✅
- **Schema — already CMS-driven.** `page?.schema_jsonld ?? composed LocalBusiness` (`:45`). ✅
- **Meta/OG — already CMS-driven.** `head` calls `pageHeadMeta(loaderData.page, "/", …)` (`:16`). ✅
- **Intro — GAP.** The designed intro renders `t.hero_subhead ?? c.tagline` (`:86`) — from `template_vars`, **not** the CMS body's searcher intro.
- **Category H2 sections + editorial internal links — THE LOAD-BEARING GAP.** The designed "Services" section renders a card grid from **`t.services`** (`template_vars`, `:117-134`) linking to **`/services/$slug`** (per-SERVICE nav cards). The CMS home `body`'s **`<h2>{category}</h2>` sections with their inline editorial `<a href="/services/{category-slug}">`** (home→CATEGORY, generated by Slice 1's home brief) **never render.** So:
  - The home passes **~no editorial authority to category pages** (nav-card links ≈ site chrome per `/seo-build` §2; the authority-passing editorial in-content links live in the un-rendered CMS body).
  - The grid also targets the **wrong tier** (per-service) vs the method's **home→category** hierarchy.
- **CMS home `body` shape** (from `aiWritePage` home brief, `body_format:"html"`): searcher intro paragraph + one `<h2>{category}</h2>` + 50–100 words each **with an inline editorial `<a>`** to the category page. `internal_links` jsonb also carries home→category `{url, anchor, context}`.

**Net:** home is SEO-hollow for the editorial-linking structure. H1/schema/meta already work; the fix is the **intro + the category H2 sections with their editorial links**, rendered inside the design.

## Options to reconcile design × CMS content

### Option A — styled CMS body block within the design
Render the CMS `home` body (via `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` + the existing `prose-ish` styling) as one section inside the bespoke page (replacing the `t.services` grid to avoid a duplicate services listing).
- **SEO:** ✅ editorial `<a>` render (authority passes), ✅ intro, H1/schema/meta already there.
- **Professional:** OK — a styled prose section within the design; less "designed" than the rest, but framed (eyebrow + section) it reads intentionally. Not a raw wall.
- **Robustness:** ✅ highest (renders the body as-is; no parsing).
- **Weakness:** the middle of the page becomes a prose block rather than the card-grid aesthetic.

### Option B — split the CMS body into DESIGNED sections [recommended]
Split the CMS `home` body on `<h2` boundaries (the same deterministic technique as Part B): the pre-first-`<h2>` chunk → feeds the **designed intro**; each `<h2>` section → rendered as a **designed category card/section** (the "What we do" grid styling), each card's inner HTML via `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` so its **inline editorial `<a>` is preserved**.
- **SEO:** ✅✅ best — H1 (hero) + intro + each category as a designed section **with its editorial in-content link** (authority passes to category pages) + schema + meta. Also **fixes the hierarchy** (home→category, replacing the wrong home→service grid).
- **Professional:** ✅✅ best — fully designed, CMS-populated (the stated ideal: "designed category sections render the CMS category H2s + their editorial `<a>`").
- **Robustness:** ✅ good — the `<h2>` split is deterministic (Slice 1's home brief guarantees `<h2>` category sections); **fallback to Option A** (styled prose block) if a body has no `<h2>` structure.
- **Weakness:** depends on the body having the intro-then-`<h2>`-sections shape (guaranteed by Slice 1; fallback covers drift).

### Option C — middle path
Bespoke hero/intro/CTA kept; source the **intro** from the CMS body's first paragraph; render the CMS body's category sections as a styled block (Option A) **in place of** the `t.services` grid; keep the rest bespoke.
- Essentially A with the intro also CMS-sourced; between A and B on "designed-ness."

## Recommendation
**Option B** — split the CMS home body on `<h2>` and render the intro into the designed intro + each category section as a designed card (preserving its inline editorial `<a>`), with a **graceful fallback to Option A** (styled prose block) when a body lacks `<h2>` structure. It is the most SEO-correct (renders the authority-passing editorial home→category links **and** fixes the wrong per-service grid target) while being the most professional (the design populated by CMS content, not a text wall). All three render H1/schema/meta (already working); B uniquely gets the editorial links into the designed structure.
